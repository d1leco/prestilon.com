Every subsystem — from signal filtering to wear prediction — is custom-built and runs entirely on your hardware. No third-party AI APIs. No data leaving your network.

01 — SIGNAL PROCESSING
📡

Kalman Filter Engine

Per-sensor Kalman filters continuously estimate true state from noisy measurements. Hotend temp, bed temp, strain gauges, motor loads — all filtered in real-time with tunable Q/R noise coefficients per subscription tier.

kalman · Q/R tuning · per-sensor
02 — CHANGE DETECTION
🔬

CUSUM Anomaly Detection

CUSUM control charts detect statistically significant shifts in process mean — catching belt wear, clog onset, and thermal runaway risk at the earliest possible moment, before symptoms are visible to the naked eye.

cusum · 5 streams · σ thresholds
03 — HEALTH SCORING
💚

AI Health Score Orchestrator

A multi-factor health score (0–100) synthesizes anomaly severity, diagnostic test results, wear model coefficients, and maintenance history through a weighted orchestration pipeline with plan-specific AI constants.

0–100 · orchestrated · tier-specific
04 — FAILURE PREDICTION
⚠️

Multi-Modal Failure Prediction

Separate prediction models for thermal runaway, belt degradation, clog probability, bed adhesion failure, and extruder wear — each outputs risk level, probability %, and estimated time to failure. Automated pause/abort signals.

shouldPause · shouldAbort · ETA

HARDWARE DIAGNOSTICS

6 tests. Real GCode.
Scored against a baseline.

PRESTILON sends actual GCode to your printer and measures the response. Every test is compared to an industry baseline and assigned a 0–100 score.

🛡️

Printer Status Check

M115 firmware query + M105 sensor read. Non-invasive — no movement. Validates Klipper connection and all sensor channels.

~5s · no movement
⚡

Extrusion Flow Test

Homes axes, raises Z to purge position, preheats to 200°C, extrudes 5mm + retracts. Measures flow consistency and pressure response.

~60–90s · physical movement
↔️

Movement & Axes Test

3× G28 X Y homing with belt tension analysis. Backlash measurement. Motor load scoring. Z axis untouched for safety.

~40–60s · XY homing ×3
🌡️

Temperature Stability

5-second continuous sampling of hotend and bed temperature variance. No GCode — pure sensor analysis against stability thresholds.

~7s · sensor only
🎯

Retraction & Pressure Test

Auto-preheats if needed, primes 3mm filament, retracts, measures pressure drop curve. Clog risk and pressure coefficient scoring.

~7s (+preheat if cold)
🏆

Full System Diagnostic

Runs all 5 tests sequentially. Produces a composite health score, fleet percentile rank, and a complete diagnostic report.

~50s idle · ~10s if busy
