// CAPABILITIES
Everything your fleet needs. Running locally.

PRESTILON installs on your Windows machine and connects directly to your printers via Moonraker/Klipper. No cloud latency. No data leaving your network. Full AI analytics, locally.

🧠

Kalman + CUSUM AI

Advanced signal filtering and change detection algorithms watch every temperature curve and motion pattern for early anomaly signals.

📊

Health Scoring

Each printer gets a continuous 0–100 health score based on diagnostics, wear models, and historical patterns. Know before it fails.

⚡

Real-time WebSocket

Live telemetry at millisecond precision. Temperature, progress, states — all streamed directly from Klipper to your dashboard.

🔮

Failure Prediction

Wear models and trend analysis predict nozzle clogs, belt degradation, and thermal issues before they cause a failed print.

🖨️

Multi-Printer Fleet

Manage up to 50 printers from one dashboard. Each with its own AI pipeline, telemetry history, and maintenance log.

🔒

Local First

Your printer data never leaves your machine. Only subscription validation uses the internet. Full privacy, full control.
