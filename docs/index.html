WHAT PRESTILON DOES

Everything your fleet needs.
Nothing you don't.

A complete AI monitoring stack — anomaly detection, failure prediction, hardware diagnostics, and autonomous maintenance — all running on your own machine.

📡

Real-Time Monitoring

Live telemetry from every printer in your fleet — temperatures, print progress, motor states — updated continuously via persistent connections.

🔬

Anomaly Detection

Statistical change detection catches belt wear, clog onset, and thermal risk at the earliest possible moment — before symptoms become visible.

💚

AI Health Score

A single 0–100 score per printer synthesizing anomaly data, diagnostic results, wear history, and maintenance records through a weighted AI pipeline.

⚠️

Failure Prediction

Separate models for thermal runaway, belt degradation, clog risk, bed adhesion, and extruder wear — each with risk level and estimated time to failure.

🤖

Autonomous Action

Three autonomy levels: passive observation, surface recommendations, or automatic corrective action execution — configurable per printer, per plan.

🔧

Hardware Diagnostics

Six real-world diagnostic tests — from firmware checks to full extrusion flow analysis — each benchmarked against a real fleet baseline.
