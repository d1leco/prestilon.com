CAPABILITIES

A full AI engine.
Not a wrapper.

Every subsystem — signal filtering to wear prediction — runs entirely on your hardware. No third-party AI APIs. No printer data leaving your network.

01 — SIGNAL PROCESSING
📡

Kalman Filter Engine

Per-sensor Kalman filters estimate true state from noisy measurements in real-time — hotend temp, bed temp, motor loads — with tunable noise coefficients per plan.

kalman · Q/R tuning · per-sensor
02 — ANOMALY DETECTION
🔬

CUSUM Change Detection

CUSUM control charts catch belt wear, clog onset, and thermal runaway risk at the earliest statistical moment — before symptoms become visible to the naked eye.

cusum · 5 streams · σ thresholds
03 — HEALTH SCORING
💚

AI Health Orchestrator

A multi-factor health score (0–100) synthesizes anomaly data, diagnostics, wear coefficients, and maintenance history through a weighted AI pipeline.

0–100 · orchestrated · tier-tuned
04 — FAILURE PREDICTION
⚠️

Multi-Modal Prediction

Separate models for thermal runaway, belt degradation, clog risk, bed adhesion, and extruder wear — each outputs risk level, probability, and time-to-failure.

shouldPause · shouldAbort · ETA
05 — AI AUTONOMY
🤖

3-Level Autonomy Control

L0 Passive — observe. L1 Recommend — surface actions. L2 Automatic — execute safe corrective GCode without approval. Per-printer override.

L0 · L1 · L2 · per-printer

HARDWARE DIAGNOSTICS

6 tests. Real GCode.
Baseline-scored results.

PRESTILON sends actual GCode to your printer and measures the response. Every result is benchmarked against a real-world fleet baseline.

Printer Status Check

M115 firmware query and M105 sensor validation. Non-invasive — no movement required.

~5s · no movement

Extrusion Flow Test
