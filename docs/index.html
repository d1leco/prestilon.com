CAPABILITIES

Enterprise AI.
Runs on your machine.

Every subsystem — from signal filtering to autonomous correction — runs entirely on your hardware. No cloud AI. No printer data leaving your network.

01 — DETECTION

Real-Time Anomaly Detection

CUSUM control charts and Kalman filtering catch belt wear, clog onset, and thermal drift before they become visible failures — at the earliest statistical moment.

cusum · kalman · 5 streams
02 — SCORING

AI Health Scoring

A per-printer score from 0–100 synthesizing anomaly severity, diagnostic results, wear models, and maintenance history through a weighted AI pipeline.

0–100 · fleet baseline · tier-tuned
03 — PREDICTION

Failure Prediction

Separate models for thermal runaway, belt degradation, clog risk, bed adhesion, and extruder wear — each with risk probability and estimated time to failure.

5 failure modes · ETA · shouldPause
04 — AUTONOMY

3-Level AI Autonomy

L0 observe. L1 recommend. L2 automatically execute safe corrective GCode — pause, fan adjust, abort — without waiting for approval.

L0 · L1 · L2 · per-printer
05 — DIAGNOSTICS

Hardware Diagnostics

Six real-world tests — from firmware status checks to full extrusion flow analysis. Each sends actual GCode and benchmarks results against a fleet baseline.

6 tests · real gcode · benchmarked
06 — TELEMETRY

WebSocket Telemetry

Persistent connections to every Moonraker instance deliver temperatures, progress, positions, and fan states in under 50ms — with automatic reconnect and stream buffering.

websocket · moonraker · <50ms
